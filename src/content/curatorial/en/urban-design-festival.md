## The Idea

A design festival that doesn't take place inside a cultural institution but along an active commercial street — twenty shops and businesses became a temporary exhibition space for twenty local designers.

## The Process

Curating here meant learning a space that is anything but neutral: every shop has its own life, opening hours, and regular customers. I met individually with every business owner to understand what was possible, then built a walking route connecting the display points into one coherent design story.

## The Result

Every stop on the route was paired with short signage inviting passersby "to step in and look," even if they had no intention of buying anything — a move that required careful coordination with the business owners.

## Reception

The festival drew roughly 3,000 visitors over a single weekend, and led to new collaborations between three of the participating designers and local businesses.
