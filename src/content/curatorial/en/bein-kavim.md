## The Idea

Eight artists working on the blurred border between two-dimensional painting and sculptural work were brought into one shared conversation: what happens when a line stops being just a line?

## The Process

The exhibition grew out of a year of studio visits and repeated readings in the local artist archive. I chose not to organize the works by artist but by visual "tension" — each room in the gallery presents one contrast: hard versus soft, transparent versus opaque.

## The Result

The spatial layout itself became part of the curatorial statement — visitors reported "reading" the show as a single narrative path rather than a separate collection of works.

## Reception

The exhibition was covered in two local art journals and extended by three weeks beyond its original run due to public demand.
