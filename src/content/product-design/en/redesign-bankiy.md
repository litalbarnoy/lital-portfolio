## The Challenge

Customers reported that the existing app "works, but feels like a form." The drop-off rate on inter-bank transfers stood at around 38%, and related support tickets were among the highest in the system. The brief: redesign the transfer experience without touching the backend core systems.

## The Process

I opened with two weeks of qualitative research — interviews with 14 users across a wide age range, and screen-recording analysis of real transfer flows. It quickly became clear the problem wasn't visual design but information architecture: users had to fill seven fields before getting any signal the transfer would succeed.

From there I moved to rapid paper prototyping, then an interactive Figma prototype tested across three rounds of iteration with real users.

## The Solution

I built a new three-step flow instead of seven, with real-time validation and a preview of the action before final confirmation. Alongside it, a design system consolidated 12 different versions of the same button into one consistent component.

## Results

Post-launch, the drop-off rate fell by 22% and transfer-related support tickets dropped by 31%, all within the original development timeline and budget.
