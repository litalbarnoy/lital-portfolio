## The Challenge

Every clinic in the network worked with a receptionist on the phone, and each had its own unwritten "protocol" for booking. The goal was to build one unified digital system serving both a young patient booking at night from their phone, and an older patient less comfortable with technology.

## The Process

I led the project from service research (a service blueprint mapping every touchpoint, from booking to the day-before reminder). I also interviewed front-desk teams at the clinics to understand which operational constraints had to show up in the booking screen.

At the prototype stage I tested two parallel design flows (A/B) with a real patient group, to decide between a fast one-step flow and a guided flow that explains each step.

## The Solution

The guided flow clearly won with the older age group, so an adaptive booking screen was built that detects the treatment type and adjusts the amount of explanation accordingly. I also added a one-tap "book again" mode for repeat patients.

## Results

Within three months of launch, 64% of appointments were booked through the digital system instead of by phone, and no-show rates dropped thanks to the smart reminders built into the flow.
