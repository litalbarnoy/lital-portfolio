## The Challenge

The operations team worked across seven different tools in parallel just to close a single process. The brief was to unify the critical information into one screen, without losing the depth the experienced team was used to.

## The Process

I spent a full week sitting physically alongside operations reps, observing their work in real time — a shorthand ethnographic method that surfaced dozens of workarounds the team had built to cope with the existing tools. I translated the findings into an information architecture that classified every data point by frequency of use.

## The Solution

The dashboard that was built shows the three most critical metrics by default, with a one-click "drill down" option for every additional metric — instead of showing everything at once. Every component was built as a live code library the engineering team could use directly.

## Results

Average handling time for the process dropped by 40%, and for the first time, the operations team itself requested additions to the system instead of complaining about it.
